Scope: the Soufflé marketing site (`site/src/index.njk`, its partials, `docs.njk`, `css/site.css`). Visitor mode: Persuade. The docs page is the same sheet at a smaller billing.

Audience: professionals who cannot hand a recording to a third party, and privacy-first developers who read the source before installing. Both arrive looking for a reason to disqualify the tool. Action: download the .dmg (or copy the brew line). Proof: the animated app mocks, which are the only demonstration this site is allowed to make — there is no social proof of any kind and none may be invented.

Constraints: the name, the logo, and the warm amber chromatic family are pinned brand commitments. The five animated mocks (`_includes/mocks/mocks.njk`, `css/mocks.css`, `js/mocks.js`) and their reduced-motion finished states are preserved as-is. EN/FR parity in `_data/*.json`; French copy runs longer and headline breaks are authored per locale. Existing copy is confirmed good and is reused, minus the eyebrows the craft floor bans.

## Direction contract

THESIS: On a page where nothing is pictured, the only picture is the software actually running. The site is a one-sheet billing poster whose hierarchy is carried by size and line breaks alone. It refuses the arrangement this category always ships and that the previous site was: centred hero over a radial glow, alternating zig-zag feature rows, trios of icon-tile cards, a gradient CTA box.

OWN-WORLD: A drenched amber sheet, edge to edge, inverting to paper cream for the dense factual foot — a handbill printed both sides. Exactly four inks: amber ground, near-black ink, oxblood second ink, paper cream. No gradient anywhere; any intermediate tone is a mechanical halftone screen. One condensed variable grotesk (Archivo, wght + wdth) does every typographic job at eight sizes; JetBrains Mono only where there is a real command, size, or timecode. Zero border-radius, zero shadow, zero glass. Rules are hairlines. The active state is inversion: fill the ink, flip the text to the ground. Buttons are rectangles with a hairline or a solid fill, never pills. No filled panel ever sits behind body text.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport that this transcribes meetings and dictation on a Mac and that the audio never leaves it. They believe it because the page shows the software running rather than describing it, and because every claim is one a sceptic can check in the source or on their own machine. They download the .dmg or copy the brew line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: No floating nav bar. A thin marginalia run across the very top — wordmark left, then the acts of the page as plain words, then EN/FR — closed by a hairline. Under it the headline at poster scale, spanning the full measure, breaking exactly where the poster wants it: three lines, tightest leading, near-black on amber. The one-line sub sits at the next billing size to the left, and the download sits right of it set as type over a hairline rule with the brew command beneath it in mono. Then, immediately and at full measure, the live meeting mock — the only image the page contains.

FORM: The Billing, candidate 4 of my ordered grounded list, assigned by the roll. Seed key bb6a84f2. Raised before presentation by four declined challengers: a closed four-ink palette with a halftone screen instead of every gradient (Game Boy field); two speakers as two exactly-registered inks rather than coloured labels on a card (risograph); a strict non-skippable running order carried by size, not printed numerals (darkroom bay); and the third value confined to edges and hairlines, never a filled panel behind text (iridescent cloud edge). The converged festival lineup poster donated billing-as-navigation, the justified tail block, struck rules for spent states, and size-step motion. Signature interaction: the billing size-step — the act in play takes top billing and its neighbours demote a size; under reduced motion rank is shown by the struck rule alone. Code-led: no image generation in this session.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
